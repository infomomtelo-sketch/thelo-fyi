Terms — thelo.fyi

What thelo.fyi is

thelo.fyi is a listing platform. We publish facility information that operators submit. We are not a licensed care facility, not a healthcare provider, and not a placement or referral agency. We do not arrange placements, do not recommend facilities, and do not screen residents for suitability.

How we are paid

Operators pay a flat monthly fee for a listing. We are never paid a commission, finder's fee, or any amount tied to a resident being admitted. We accept no payment from families or placement agents.

Operator responsibilities

By listing, you confirm you have authority to list the facility and that the information you submit is public facility information — no resident records, health information, or personal identifiers. You are responsible for keeping availability and pricing current.

Accuracy

Listings are provided as submitted. We do not independently verify bed counts, pricing, or room availability. Licensing information is taken from the public CDSS file on the date shown and may change at any time. Confirm licensing status directly with CDSS before making any placement decision.

No compliance determination

Nothing on thelo.fyi is a compliance finding. thelo.fyi supplements, and does not replace, your facility's own licensing and record-keeping obligations. Compliance is determined solely by CDSS.

Transportation workflows, PHI, and BAA

Transportation workflow features are designed for scheduling logistics only and public facility operations data. Do not submit resident names, medical details, diagnosis, insurance, or other protected health information through thelo.fyi unless a signed Business Associate Agreement is in place for that specific integration.

Any PHI-enabled integration may be offered only after legal approval and a signed BAA with the relevant vendor and scope.

Provided "as is"

The service is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
